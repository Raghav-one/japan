Airport choice

HND: generally more convenient for central Tokyo; airport rail reaches central areas in roughly 30 minutes, then add hotel connection, queues and walking. NRT: Skyliner suits Ueno/Nippori; Narita Express suits Tokyo/Shinagawa/Shinjuku. Allow approximately 60–100 minutes onward depending on destination and service. Prefer a daytime arrival over a modestly cheaper late arrival. Transfer costs below are estimates. [8]

Booking filter

Prefer arrival by early/mid-afternoon on day 2 and a day-7 flight reaching Chennai the same calendar day. Aim for a protected 2–4-hour layover, subject to airline minimum connections. Reject an involuntary airport change or overnight connection unless deliberately chosen. Japan is 3½ hours ahead of IST; all tickets show local times.

Pre-payment flight checklist & transit traps
  • Search all three passengers together; the lowest fare bucket may not have three seats. Compare airline-direct all-in price, not search snippets. Record flight numbers, dates, elapsed time, baggage and refund/change costs.
  • Names must match passports, including given/surname handling. Verify passport expiry, blank pages and transit-country requirements with the carrier; do not apply a universal six-month rule to every country.
  • One protected ticket is preferable to separate tickets. A booking reference alone does not prove connection protection: ask who rebooks you after a missed connection and whether bags reach Japan.
  • For Singapore, collecting bags or checking in landside means entering Singapore and meeting entry requirements. Airside transit and entering Singapore are different; do not assume a Japan visa automatically grants Singapore entry. Ask the airline to confirm the exact transfer. [9]
  • Check cabin kg/pieces, checked kg/pieces on every segment, seat fees, refund deductions, no-show penalties and schedule-change rights. Shopping return baggage matters.
  • Get itemised cancellation terms. “Refundable” can still mean deductions. Visa refusal is not automatically covered by airline or insurance. Save the fare rules and payment receipt.

03 · Passport, visa and arrival: sort these before locking costs

Critical timing conflict: Chennai VFS says applications can start only 89 days before travel, and its tourist checklist recommends reservations rather than confirmed non-refundable travel before visa approval. For a 15 February departure, 89 days before is 18 November 2026—after your 10 November purchase deadline. For 1 February it is 4 November; for 28 February it is 1 December. Buy a genuinely suitable refundable/changeable fare or consciously accept visa-related loss; do not use a fake booking. [1–2]

Swaps: theme park, snow, onsen, Fuji overnight and two-city route

Accommodation shortlist strategy

Compare a true triple, an apartment-style room and two rooms. Apartment-hotel brands can be search starting points, not availability endorsements. Default: one IC card/device per person, pay per ride; reserve excursion trains separately. IC cards are stored value, not unlimited passes. A physical Suica/PASMO-compatible card is the safe fallback for incompatible phones. JR East’s Welcome Suica Mobile is currently iOS; Samsung support is announced for first half 2027 but should not be assumed ready in February. [10]

Operational rules that save time
  • Subway passes do not automatically cover JR, private lines or every airport service. Compare the specific day’s eligible rides before buying a 24/48/72-hour pass.
  • Limited express / Shinkansen can need both fare and reserved-seat entitlement. A normal IC tap alone is not the whole ticket. Avoid accidental duplicate payment when linking IC cards.
  • Tokaido/Sanyo/Kyushu Shinkansen luggage over 160 cm and up to 250 cm summed dimensions generally needs the appropriate oversized-baggage arrangement; verify current product/seat rules. Over 250 cm is not allowed under normal luggage rules. Reserve three seats together where possible. [13]
  • Check the actual last train, not a generic “midnight” rule. Taxi fares and luggage capacity vary; three adults plus three large bags may need a larger vehicle. Budget night transfers deliberately.
  • Use station exits and platform signs, allow 10–20 minutes for large stations, carry bags rather than blocking escalators, and keep tickets through exit gates. Reserve lifts if accessibility matters.

09 · Food, cash, data & everyday use

Food and dietary constraints

Budget meals ¥700–1,200; casual sit-down ¥1,200–2,500; nicer dinner ¥3,000–6,000+ per person, estimates. Add drinks, café stops and possible table charges. Try ramen, curry, sushi, tempura, grilled foods, bakery and department-store food halls; ask what the broth contains. Do not build the itinerary around long restaurant queues.

Vegetarian food can contain fish-based dashi, bonito or meat stock; removing meat is insufficient. Confirm seafood, egg, dairy, pork and alcohol restrictions for all three. Carry a Japanese allergy card listing the precise allergen and severity; translation alone does not guarantee safe preparation. Halal/vegan needs require restaurant-level verification. Water is generally available; carry a refill bottle. Tipping is not customary. Queue, use payment trays where provided and keep voices low on trains.

Money

Carry two independent payment options, ideally different issuers, with international usage enabled. Keep one backup separate. Start with roughly ¥15,000–25,000 cash per person as an optional working float, not an entry requirement; use ATMs as needed. Check ATM issuer/operator fees, daily limits and hotel preauthorisation capacity. Pick JPY at payment/ATM to avoid dynamic currency conversion; your bank still sets its own FX rate and fees. Do not assume UPI is widely accepted.

Connectivity and apps

Check every phone is unlocked and supports the selected eSIM; confirm data size, hotspot, activation trigger, validity and Japanese network. Data-only eSIMs may not make ordinary emergency/voice calls. Keep one Indian line reachable for OTP/airline/eVISA; compare roaming fees. Pocket Wi-Fi is shared but separates poorly when the group splits and needs return/deposit handling.

Install Google Maps, offline Japanese translation, airline app, chosen rail tools and Safety tips. Save hotel address in Japanese, tickets and insurer details offline. Test eSIM setup before departure; activate according to provider instructions. Bring a power bank under the airline’s current cabin-only battery rules.

10 · Health, winter, safety & packing

Japan emergency: police 110 · ambulance/fire 119 · JNTO visitor hotline 050-3816-2787, 24 hours. From abroad use +81 and omit the leading 0 for the hotline. Data-only SIM users need a viable calling method. [16]
Medicines and insurance

Check each medicine by active ingredient, not Indian brand. Japan restricts some stimulants/narcotics and other controlled medicines; an Indian prescription is not blanket permission. MHLW permits up to one month of ordinary prescription medicine without Import Confirmation in applicable cases, but controlled substances have separate rules/advance permissions. Keep labelled packaging and prescription. Resolve exceptions before departure. [17]

Buy overseas medical/evacuation coverage for actual dates, including transit and a buffer. Declare relevant conditions; compare deductibles, hospital assistance, trip interruption, baggage and cancellation exclusions. Winter sports need explicit cover. Insurance is strongly recommended here; no universal mandatory-policy entry claim is made.

Winter packing from Chennai

Layer: thermal base + sweater/fleece + insulated wind-resistant coat; gloves, scarf, warm socks, beanie, grippy comfortable shoes and compact umbrella. Mountain day needs more warmth; avoid slick soles on ice. Plan for 10,000–18,000 steps on busy days only if the group is comfortable; schedule café breaks. Pack lip balm, moisturiser, sunscreen, personal medicines and a small daypack.

Japan uses 100V power and mainly type-A plugs. Check every charger label for 100–240V; a plug adapter does not convert voltage. Avoid taking a 230V-only heating appliance. Leave suitcase space and weigh bags before the return flight.

Behaviour, disruptions and accessibility

Carry original passport while out; protect it from loss and retain secure copies separately. Avoid street touts and unpriced nightlife venues; keep valuables attended. Follow designated smoking areas and local photo signs, ask before photographing people, avoid blocking pavements and take rubbish to appropriate bins. Eating while walking depends on location; follow venue practice.

For earthquakes, protect your head, follow staff/official guidance and avoid lifts during an event. Coastal tsunami warnings require moving to designated higher ground. Follow JMA alerts and rail notices; do not travel into a warned area for sightseeing. Safety tips provides alerts. Ask hotels about lifts, step-free station access, bed height and bathing needs if anyone has mobility constraints. Onsen commonly requires washing before bathing and may have tattoo restrictions; verify the specific venue.
